— Our Story

About MRVConsultants

MRVConsultants is an educational guidance and student support consultancy based in Vavuniya, Sri Lanka. We provide guidance to students who are interested in language classes, overseas education opportunities, and the student visa preparation process.

Our goal is to help students make informed decisions about their international education journey by providing reliable information, personalized guidance, and continuous support at different stages of the process.
